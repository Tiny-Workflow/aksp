A LITTLE IDEA, A LOT OF HEART

How a Class 9E school mela stall became an adda for desi flavours and good vibes.

💛 ✳ 🫶
CHAPTER 01 — THE IDEA
💡

It started with a simple thought.

Every school mela needs that one spot where friends meet, laughter happens, and someone always says, "Bhai, ek aur le lete hain!" We wanted to create exactly that.

So came Aao Kabhi Stall Pe! — a playful little food-stall idea built around familiar flavours, colourful vibes, and the joy of sharing a treat with your friends.

CHAPTER 02 — THE VIBE
🇮🇳

Desi dil. Dilli wali vibe.

Our theme is inspired by the energy of Indian street-food culture: bold colours, fun words, chatpata snacks and a whole lot of personality.

The idea is to make the stall feel friendly and fun — a place for pet puja, dosti, and memories from the school mela.

CHAPTER 03 — OUR MISSION
🫶

More than a menu.

Behind the food is a team learning to plan, work together, present an idea, and turn a classroom project into something people can enjoy.

Every little detail adds to the experience. We're here to celebrate teamwork, creativity and the happy chaos of a school mela.

OUR MOTTO

Pet bhi khush,
dil bhi khush!

Come hungry. Leave happy. Make a memory.
